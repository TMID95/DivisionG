Division at a glance

What's happening in Division G

Denmark, Sweden & Norway
Four areas · clubs from Copenhagen to Oslo

Division G is the Nordic home of District 95. Clubs meet in English, Danish, Swedish and Norwegian — in Copenhagen, across Jutland and Funen, in southern Sweden, and in Oslo and Rogaland. Visit as a guest at any club before you join.

The four areas

  • Area G1 — Greater Copenhagen
  • Area G2 — Jutland & Funen
  • Area G3 — Southern Sweden & CPH City
  • Area G4 — Norway

What is Toastmasters?

Toastmasters International is a world-leading communication and leadership organisation with over 300,000 members in 145 countries. Members grow their confidence, sharpen their public speaking skills, and discover their voice — in a supportive, non-judgmental community.
